Inside the app

Four screens. Nothing you don't need.

Vote

Your ballot queue

The open questions for your city, province and country — the ones you're actually eligible for, nothing else.

Results

Published counts

Every ballot that has passed the threshold, with its split and its entry on the public record.

Orgs

Your groups

Private ballots for any organization you belong to — a union local, a board, a community association.

Identity

Your credential

Your verification status and region, and the history of every ballot you've cast.

Getting started

About three minutes, once.

1

Download and sign in

Apple or Google. No password to invent.

2

A government ID and a short selfie check, about three minutes. Free, and your documents are never stored by Represent.

3

Vote

Your region comes from your ID, so the questions you see are the ones you're entitled to answer.
