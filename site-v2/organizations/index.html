Why it matters

Most of the cost is the argument afterwards.

The expensive part of an internal vote isn't running it. It's the fortnight afterwards, when somebody claims the count was wrong, the turnout was unrepresentative, or people voted twice.

A ballot where every voter is a verified member, each counted once, with an exportable signed record, gives you something concrete to answer that with. That's what you're buying.
